Artificial Intelligence (AI) is the simulation of human intelligence in machines.
Machine Learning (ML) is a subset of AI that enables systems to learn from data.
Deep Learning uses neural networks with many layers to learn complex patterns.
Large Language Models (LLMs) like GPT-4 are trained on vast amounts of text data.
Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) combines a retrieval system with a language model.
In RAG, relevant documents are fetched from a vector database before generating an answer.
ChromaDB is an open-source vector database that stores and retrieves embeddings locally.
Embeddings are numerical representations of text that capture semantic meaning.
The BAAI/bge-small-en-v1.5 model generates high quality embeddings for free locally.
OpenAI's gpt-4o-mini is a cost-effective model for generating high quality answers.
